# Case study 16: XP School Doncaster and the XP Trust

**Scope.** XP School (opened September 2014, free school; founders Gwyn ap Harri and Andy Sprakes) is a deliberately small 11–19 school of ~245–250 pupils on Doncaster's Middle Bank, modelled explicitly on the US EL Education (Expeditionary Learning) network and High Tech High ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XP_School); [Edge Foundation case study](https://www.edge.co.uk/practice/practice-library/projects/XP-School-Doncaster/)). It has grown into the XP Trust — eight schools as of 2025: XP, XP East (same campus), XP Gateshead, and five Doncaster primaries (Carcroft, Green Top, Plover, Norton Juniors, Norton Infants) ([XP Trust — Our Schools](https://xptrust.org/our-schools/)). It is the most complete UK implementation of expeditionary learning, crew and standards-based habits assessment — and its published exam data now provide the most honest stress-test of that model in an English accountability context.

## What the evidence says

**The underlying US model has genuinely strong evidence.** Mathematica's quasi-experimental evaluation of five EL Education middle schools (Washington DC and NYC, matched-comparison design) found reading gains equivalent to ~5 extra months of learning after two years and ~7 months after three; maths gains of ~3 months after two years and ~10 months after three ([Mathematica/EL Education, 2013](https://eleducation.org/news/mathematica-policy-research-releases-evaluation-expeditionary-learning-middle-schools); [ERIC ED618299](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED618299)). Note this is the *whole model* — expeditions plus crew plus intensive teacher coaching — not project-based learning alone.

**PBL alone, transplanted into English secondaries, has failed its best test.** The EEF's randomised trial of "Learning through REAL Projects" (the same High Tech High-derived PBL that inspired XP, at 20–50% of timetable) found **no evidence of positive impact on literacy or engagement, and a possible *negative* impact on literacy for FSM-eligible pupils**; five schools dropped out and attrition weakens the finding, but it remains the best UK causal evidence ([EEF trial page](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/projects/project-based-learning); trad-side critique: [Ashman, 2016](https://gregashman.wordpress.com/2016/11/05/project-based-learning-fails-a-key-test/)). The honest reading: PBL as a bolt-on is risky for disadvantaged learners; the EL results suggest it can work only as a whole-school culture with heavy teacher development.

**Crew has striking (but weak-design) relational evidence.** The Relational Schools Foundation's "We are Crew" study (533 questionnaires, two weeks after Y7s met their crews on the Outward Bound residential) found teacher-rated relationships with students at 83% relational proximity (+25pp vs benchmark), student–teacher at 78% (+16pp) and peer-to-peer at 64% (+19pp) ([Relational Schools, 2017](https://relationalschools.org/2017/10/19/crew-building-relationships-expeditionary-learning-xp-school/)). This is correlational, single-school, and short-horizon — treat as promising, not proof. It is, however, consistent with EEF Toolkit evidence that social and emotional learning approaches add ~4 months on average, with larger potential benefits for disadvantaged pupils ([EEF Toolkit: SEL](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/social-and-emotional-learning)).

**Ofsted evidence: culture outstanding, academic delivery contested.** XP was rated Outstanding in 2017, then **Good in June 2023** — with Behaviour & Attitudes and Personal Development both still **Outstanding**, but Quality of Education downgraded because older pupils had "significant gaps in subject knowledge" and some were inadequately prepared for GCSEs; the school has since added discrete, specialist maths teaching alongside expeditions ([Ofsted history](https://reports.ofsted.gov.uk/provider/23/140964); [2023 report PDF](https://files.ofsted.gov.uk/v1/file/50228322)). Sister school **XP East was rated Requires Improvement in June 2024** — again with Outstanding behaviour and personal development and a Good sixth form ([Ofsted: XP East](https://reports.ofsted.gov.uk/provider/23/143916)). The pattern across both inspections is identical and diagnostic: the crew/culture layer works exceptionally; the expeditionary curriculum layer has struggled to deliver secure, sequenced subject knowledge at GCSE.

**Results evidence points the same way.** First-cohort euphoria (2019: P8 +0.21, A8 52.1) gave way to post-COVID decline: P8 **−0.77 (2022)** and **−0.59 (2023)**, A8 41.2 (2023), though grade 5+ in English & maths recovered to 43% in 2023 ([Snobe/DfE data](https://snobe.co.uk/schools/xp-school); [DfE compare-school-performance](https://www.compare-school-performance.service.gov.uk/school/140964/xp-school/secondary)). Destinations are also weaker than the rhetoric: 2023 sustained education/employment was reported at ~85% (74% FE, 6% apprenticeships) — several points below typical national rates (~94% for mainstream schools; verify against the latest DfE destinations release). Cohorts are tiny (50/year), so single-year P8 swings of ±0.3 are within noise — but three consecutive below-average years is a trend, not noise.

## What the best operators actually do

- **Expeditions with a spine.** Cross-disciplinary expeditions (STEAM or Humanities blocks) built around a real-world "driving question", ending in a **public product** — exhibition, book, presentation — with genuine external audiences ([Edge Foundation](https://www.edge.co.uk/practice/practice-library/projects/XP-School-Doncaster/)). Post-2023, XP itself re-inserted **discrete specialist teaching (notably maths)** alongside expeditions — the operators' own correction is the key lesson.
- **Crew as the primary pastoral unit.** Every student in a small crew with one staff member, meeting daily for reflection, mutual accountability and academic mentoring; Year 7 starts with a week-long Outward Bound residential in the Brecon Beacons that front-loads relationship capital ([Edge](https://www.edge.co.uk/practice/practice-library/projects/XP-School-Doncaster/); [Relational Schools](https://relationalschools.org/2017/10/19/crew-building-relationships-expeditionary-learning-xp-school/)). "We are Crew" is the trust's organising sentence for staff and governance too.
- **Flat subject hierarchy, no uniform.** Subjects carry equal status inside expeditions (science exhibited as art, writing assessed in humanities products); the school also has no uniform — belonging is built through crew and shared work, not dress ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XP_School)).
- **Standards-based assessment of character and work.** HoWLs — "Work Hard, Get Smart, Be Kind" — are rubric-assessed habits reported alongside academic learning targets ([XP Trust HoWLs](https://www.xptrust.org/about-us/how-we-xp/our-habits-of-work-and-learning/)). Students defend progress in **student-led conferences**, presenting portfolios and evidence against learning targets and HoWLs to parents and teachers ([Gesher School account of XP SLCs](https://gesherschool.com/student-led-conferences-at-xp-school-doncaster/)).
- **Deliberately small, deliberately mixed.** 50 pupils per year, admitted by **random lottery run independently by the LA, with fair banding** (non-verbal reasoning test into five ability bands) so the intake mirrors Doncaster ([XP admissions](https://xpschool.org/about-us/how-to-apply-for-a-place-at-xp/)). Staff:student ratio ~1:10 vs ~1:20 LA average ([Snobe](https://snobe.co.uk/schools/xp-school)).

**Contested/weak evidence flags:** tiny cohorts make XP's P8 volatile; the crew study is self-commissioned and short-horizon; XP's destination claims outrun the published data; the EEF PBL trial had high attrition; and no independent UK evaluation of the full XP model exists. Replication context has also changed: XP entered via the free-schools programme, whose mainstream wave is paused under the current government — replication today means trust growth or in-school adoption, and from November 2025 schools face Ofsted report cards, whose multi-domain format arguably *favours* XP's profile (top marks on personal development/behaviour shown separately from attainment).

## Design implications for our school

1. **Adopt crew wholesale; adopt expeditions selectively.** Crew (daily, small, one consistent adult, 5-year relationship) is XP's most defensible export for a high-deprivation, high-SEND intake — it is cheap relative to impact and aligns with EEF SEL/mentoring evidence. Expeditionary PBL is the risky half: the EEF trial suggests it can *harm* FSM pupils' literacy if it displaces explicit teaching.
2. **Hybrid curriculum architecture from day one.** Do what XP was forced into after 2023: discrete, specialist, explicitly-taught maths, English and science spine (Rosenshine-consistent), with expeditions as the *application and motivation* layer (say 20–30% of timetable), not the delivery mechanism for core knowledge.
3. **Keep the public-product discipline.** Exhibitions, books and presentations of learning to real audiences drive redrafting, pride and attendance for disengaged learners — retain these even inside a knowledge-rich curriculum.
4. **Front-load belonging: Year 7 residential in week one.** The relational-proximity data, though weak-design, plus XP's twice-repeated Outstanding behaviour/personal-development judgements, justify budgeting for an induction residential for every child (with SEND-adapted provision), funded via pupil premium.
5. **Standards-based HoWLs reporting and student-led conferences** give SEND and low-prior-attainment pupils a success currency that GCSE grades alone deny them — but pair them with rigorous, comparable academic assessment so gaps are visible early (XP's Ofsted downgrade came from gaps discovered late).
6. **Small-school feel without small-school economics.** XP's 50/year, 1:10 ratio is financially unreplicable at scale; engineer smallness instead via schools-within-a-school (crews of ~12, year-halls of ~60–90) inside a viable 6–8FE structure.
7. **Fair banding + lottery admissions** is worth copying for a comprehensive intake in a polarised catchment.
8. **Set honest accountability tripwires.** Commit publicly to P8 ≥ 0 and national-average EBacc/destinations within four years of first cohort; XP shows culture metrics can stay outstanding while exam outcomes drift for three consecutive years — don't let the former mask the latter.
