# Case study 14: Star Academies (formerly Tauheedul Education Trust)

*How a Blackburn-born trust gets some of England's best Progress 8 scores in some of its poorest wards — and where the model has limits.*

## What the evidence says

Star Academies grew out of Tauheedul Islam Girls' High School (TIGHS) in Blackburn, one of England's most deprived boroughs. Sir Hamid Patel — a Blackburn-raised Islamic scholar who left school at 16 — has been chief executive since the trust's formation in 2010; it rebranded from "Tauheedul Education Trust" to "Star Academies" in 2017 as it expanded beyond Islamic-ethos schools into non-faith and Christian schools ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Academies); [ummid profile](https://ummid.com/news/2025/march/17-03-2025/meet-sir-hamid-patel-first-british-muslim-education-chair-in-uk.html)). Today it runs ~36 schools across the North West, Yorkshire, Midlands and London, of which about 21 have a Muslim religious character; Patel was knighted in 2021 and became interim chair of Ofsted in 2025 ([Star Academies](https://staracademies.org/about-us/star-academies/); [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/new-ofsted-chair-hamid-patel-said-trojan-horse-alienated-muslims-denied-claims-takeover-plot)).

The headline numbers are extraordinary and repeatedly verified in DfE performance tables. In 2018 the trust posted the highest MAT Progress 8 in England (+1.42); in 2019 it topped the MAT tables again at +0.74, with TIGHS first nationally (P8 = 2.16) and Star schools taking the top three P8 places in the country ([Star, 2020](https://staracademies.org/news-story/star-academies-top-of-the-class-in-mat-performance-tables/)). In 2024 (the last year P8 could be calculated — no KS2 baseline exists for 2024/25 and 2025/26 cohorts due to COVID), three Star schools were again in England's top ten for P8 ([DfE secondary accountability guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/progress-8-school-performance-measure/secondary-accountability-measures-2025-guidance-for-maintained-secondary-schools-academies-and-free-schools); [ummid](https://ummid.com/news/2025/march/17-03-2025/meet-sir-hamid-patel-first-british-muslim-education-chair-in-uk.html)).

Crucially, the results hold for *disadvantaged* pupils specifically, and survive contextualisation. In 2024, Eden Girls' Leadership Academy Manchester recorded P8 of +0.92 for its 55 disadvantaged pupils and Eden Boys' Leadership Academy Birmingham East +0.89 for 46 ([Curriculum substack analysis of DfE data](https://curriculum.substack.com/p/top-93-schools-for-disadvantaged)). The University of Bristol / Northern Powerhouse Partnership **Fairer Schools Index** — a contextually adjusted alternative to P8 built precisely to correct P8's bias against deprived intakes — placed six Star secondaries in England's top 50 in 2025, with three in the top seven ([NPP](https://www.northernpowerhousepartnership.co.uk/northern-powerhouse-launches-third-edition-of-fairer-schools-index/); [Star 2025 review](https://staracademies.org/news-story/2025-our-year-in-review/)).

**Honest caveats.** P8 flatters schools with high-EAL, high-aspiration ethnic-minority intakes, and research finds faith-school P8 falls "dramatically" once pupil characteristics are adjusted for ([Local Schools Network summary](https://www.localschoolsnetwork.org.uk/2019/10/progress-8-measure-can-mislead-parents-and-should-be-taken-with-a-pinch-of-salt); [EPI on P8](https://epi.org.uk/publications-and-research/analysis-introduction-progress-8/)). Star's flagship schools are oversubscribed single-sex Muslim-ethos free schools whose families self-select for engagement — poor, but not typical of, say, a white working-class coastal catchment. The clean test is its non-faith takeovers, and there the record is genuinely mixed: Highfield Leadership Academy (Blackpool), taken over in 2016, went into special measures in 2019 with P8 of −0.95 ([Schools Week](https://schoolsweek.co.uk/first-non-faith-school-inspected-at-star-academies-trust-put-in-special-measures/)) and was still Requires Improvement at its March 2024 inspection ([Ofsted](https://reports.ofsted.gov.uk/provider/23/142469)). The Valley Leadership Academy saw NEU strikes in 2025 over "virtual maths teachers" taught remotely from Devon ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Academies)). The faith start-up template exports imperfectly; the culture-and-systems machinery still transfers better than most (all eight Star schools inspected in 2025 sustained or improved judgements, and its first two schools under Ofsted's new report cards scored 'Exceptional'/'Strong standard' across the board — [Star performance page](https://staracademies.org/education/our-performance/)).

## What the best operators actually do

1. **Attendance as the foundation stone.** Star schools "consistently secure the lowest absence rates in England" and regularly top DfE attendance tables ([Star](https://staracademies.org/education/our-performance/)) against a national absence rate of ~6.9% in 2024/25 ([DfE](https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/pupil-absence-in-schools-in-england/2024-25-autumn-term)). Attendance is not a compliance metric but a *rewarded virtue*, wired into the pupil award structure (below).
2. **A codified character-and-service economy.** The **Tauheedul Baccalaureate / Star Diploma** awards Bronze→Platinum at KS3/4 for the "ABC": Attendance, Behaviour and Community service ([TIGHS](https://www.tighs.com/education/tauheedul-baccalaureate/); [TIBHS](https://www.tibhs.com/education/star-diploma/)). Trust-wide STAR values (Service, Teamwork, Ambition, Respect) are mapped to British values; pupils raised £350,000 for charity in 2024–25 ([Star](https://staracademies.org/news-story/2025-our-year-in-review/)). Every school carries a **Leadership specialism**, with an R-18 Leadership Framework running Reception to KS5 ([TIGHS](https://www.tighs.com/education/leadership-specialism/)).
3. **A knowledge-rich, EBacc-heavy academic core for everyone.** Core Knowledge Sequence UK at primary; a "traditional learning" secondary curriculum where nearly all pupils take EBacc subjects — 89.3% EBacc entry vs 40% nationally in 2019 — with GCSE content deliberately spread across Years 10–11 ([Star curriculum](https://staracademies.org/education/our-curriculum/); [Star 2020](https://staracademies.org/news-story/star-academies-top-of-the-class-in-mat-performance-tables/)). This is EEF-consistent: the biggest levers for disadvantaged pupils are teaching quality and curriculum breadth, not intervention bolt-ons.
4. **A grow-your-own leadership pipeline at industrial scale.** Star Talent Academy / Star Institute delivers NPQs (with the National Institute of Teaching), induction for new senior leaders, an Exemplary Leadership Programme, and "Star Excellence Professional Pathways" career frameworks; the trust runs three Teaching School Hubs supporting **1,000+ schools** across seven local authorities ([Star L&D](https://staracademies.org/working-at-star/learning-and-development/); [TIGHS hubs](https://www.tighs.com/news-story/three-teaching-school-hubs-for-star-schools/)). Leadership pipeline is the trust's stated expansion constraint-breaker.
5. **Template replication, then sponsorship.** The expansion playbook: open new single-sex "Eden Boys'/Eden Girls' Leadership Academy" free schools to a tested blueprint in deprived urban wards (Birmingham, Coventry, Manchester, Slough, Waltham Forest), and separately sponsor failing schools. The Eton College partnership planned three selective "Eton Star" sixth-form colleges; after Labour's October 2024 pause of 44 free-school projects, Dudley and Oldham were approved in 2025 but Middlesbrough was cancelled ([Which School Advisor](https://whichschooladvisor.com/uk/school-news/will-etons-state-sixth-forms-open-middlesbrough-oldham-dudley-still-on-hold); [Eton](https://www.etoncollege.com/news-and-diary/school-news/eton-and-star-academies-to-open-sixth-form-colleges-in-dudley-middlesbrough-and-oldham/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Academies)).

## Design implications for our school

- **Make attendance the first KPI and a celebrated virtue, not a sanction regime.** Star's single most transferable habit; in a high-deprivation catchment attendance is upstream of everything.
- **Codify character.** Build a Bacc-style award (Bronze→Platinum) on Attendance + Behaviour + Community service, badged and celebrated each term. It converts ethos into a visible, earnable currency — powerful for SEND pupils, whose success criteria it broadens beyond attainment.
- **Serve, don't just receive.** Star pupils *give* (£350k raised, civic leadership roles). Positioning deprived pupils as contributors builds the belonging and purpose the wellbeing literature calls for.
- **Hold the academic line.** Knowledge-rich, EBacc-core curriculum for nearly all, with vocational routes as designed pathways not dumping grounds. Do not lower the ceiling because the intake is poor.
- **Build the leadership pipeline before you need it.** In-house institute, NPQ delivery, defined career pathways, leadership induction. Recruitment in deprived areas is solved by growing, not buying, leaders.
- **Copy the humility, not just the highlights.** Star's start-up template works; its coastal white-working-class turnaround (Blackpool) has taken 8+ years and isn't finished. Assume legacy-culture change is a 5–10 year project and resource it accordingly.
- **Interpret P8 with context.** Use contextualised measures (Fairer Schools Index-style) internally; our SEND-heavy intake will be punished by raw P8 just as Star's ethos-selected intake is flattered by it.
- **Mind the 2025–26 policy weather.** Free-school route is paused/reviewed; new provision is likelier to be approved for SEND/AP — which fits our brief ([Which School Advisor](https://whichschooladvisor.com/uk/school-news/will-etons-state-sixth-forms-open-middlesbrough-oldham-dudley-still-on-hold)).
