# Section 0 — Canon: The Single Source of Truth

*This file fixes the load-bearing numbers that every other section must quote. Where a section currently contradicts this file, the section is wrong and must be conformed. Rationale and arithmetic are shown so the decisions can be defended, not just asserted.*

## 1. Age range and size

**The school is all-through 2–18.** This is forced by Section 5's 2-year-old nursery and Best Start Family Hub bid: a 2-year-old intake means the school is 2–18, not 3–18 (Section 3's error) or 11–16 (Section 6's costing error). The relational "cradle-to-career" premise is the whole reason for the all-through structure, so we keep the youngest entry point and make the rest of the document consistent with it.

**Forms of entry, per phase:**
- **Nursery (age 2–4):** ~50 funded early-years places (part-time, on early-years hourly rates), on the Reach model.
- **Primary (Reception–Year 6):** **2FE, 60/year → 420 pupils.**
- **Secondary (Year 7–11):** **6FE, 180/year → 900 pupils.**
- **Sixth form (Year 12–13):** **150 places** (a real phase — see the post-16 note below).

**Total: ~1,520 pupils on roll (statutory age), ~1,570 including nursery.** Arithmetic: 420 (primary) + 900 (secondary) + 150 (sixth form) = 1,470 + ~50 nursery. This sits inside Section 1's "1,400–1,800" range and replaces both Section 3's "180/year all-through" ambiguity and Section 6's 1,200-place 11–16 school.

**External-joiner fraction — stated honestly.** A 2FE primary (60 Year 6 pupils) feeding a 6FE secondary (180 Year 7 places) means **only one-third of each Year 7 progresses internally; two-thirds (120 of 180) arrive from external primaries.** The all-through "no Year 6→7 cliff" claim therefore applies in full only to the internal third. This is not a flaw to hide but a design fact that the transition programme (point 5) is built to answer. We chose 2FE primary over 3FE because a larger primary raises cost without changing the basic truth that most of the secondary intake is external; the honest fix is a strong transition offer, not a bigger primary.

## 2. The school day

The current "08:00–16:30 ≈ 33 hours" is arithmetically wrong: 8.5 hours × 5 = **42.5 hours**, ten hours beyond the DfE 32.5-hour expectation, unaffordable on a 2.5% pay uplift and a burnout risk. Corrected design:

- **Site open 07:45** (canonical — supersedes Section 1's "~8am" and aligns with Section 3). **Free breakfast club 07:45–08:20.**
- **Compulsory core day 08:00–15:30** (Morning Meeting 08:00–08:25). Gross 7.5 hours/day × 5 = 37.5; net of a 50-minute daily lunch = **~33.3 directed hours/week** — at, and slightly above, the 32.5-hour statutory expectation. *This* is the "~33 hours" figure; it is the compulsory core, not the whole site-open window.
- **15:30–16:30 directed-but-tiered block.** For the identified target cohort this is **compulsory, directed** high-dosage tutoring and targeted academic support; for other pupils it is a **guaranteed rotating enrichment entitlement**. Either way it is programmed and non-voluntary, so a pupil's directed week runs to ~38 hours only for the targeted cohort.
- **16:30–17:00, site closes 17:00:** genuinely voluntary homework club and clubs.

**Which slot carries the extended-time evidence claim:** the **directed 15:30–16:30 targeted-support block**. The EEF's +2 months (≈+3 for disadvantaged) attaches to *targeted, programmed* time — and, critically, we make that block **directed/compulsory for the target cohort**, because disadvantaged and SEND pupils are precisely those least likely to attend optional twilight sessions. We do **not** attribute the extended-time effect to any voluntary slot.

## 3. Funding assumptions

**Correct the Pupil Premium error.** Pupil Premium is **not** paid on the September-2026 Universal-Credit FSM expansion. It stays tied to the pre-2026 targeted eligibility (the old ≤£7,400 threshold and Ever-6 rule). Newly eligible "expanded-FSM" pupils deliver **family savings (~£500/child/year) and the NFF FSM factor only (~£295 transitional–£505 standard)** — not PP. So "pushing registration higher" is worth doing for families and for the FSM factor, but PP income must be modelled solely on the **targeted (≤£7,400 / Ever-6) cohort**, not on the ~45% headline FSM6 figure.

**Phase-correct rates (2026–27):**
- **Early years:** funded on the EY hourly formula (2-year-old entitlement; 3–4 universal/extended hours) — a separate stream, ~£0.3–0.4m for ~50 places.
- **Primary NFF:** basic entitlement **£5,115** (also the primary minimum per-pupil level); deprivation/LPA/EAL-weighted effective **~£6,000–6,800/pupil**.
- **Secondary NFF:** basic **£6,388 (KS3)/£7,018 (KS4)**; deprivation-weighted effective **~£8,200–8,800/pupil** (Section 1/7 figure, correct).
- **Primary PP:** **~£1,550** per eligible pupil (2026–27, +2.3% on 2025–26).
- **Secondary PP:** **~£1,100** per eligible pupil.
- **16–19:** ESFA 16-19 national funding formula, **£5,133/student** band 5 national base rate plus disadvantage/retention uplifts.
- **Lump sum:** NFF lump sum, **~£0.15m** per school.
- **SEND:** first £6,000/pupil from the notional SEN budget; above that, LA top-ups. Resourced-provision/SEN-unit base places attract **£6,000 per occupied pre-16 place** — the base pupil's core funding is already inside the NFF lines above; £10,000 applies only to *unoccupied* places (and AP). Any higher per-place rate is an above-tariff local negotiation, not the national tariff. High-needs NFF **frozen for 2026–27**.

This **replaces** Section 6's £11.3–11.8m, which costed only the 11–16 slice with no EYFS, primary, sixth-form or primary-PP lines, and which overstated PP by applying the secondary rate to all 540 FSM6 pupils rather than the targeted cohort. PP falls from ~£0.58m to ~£0.49m once the error is corrected.

## 4. Admissions position

- **PAN per phase:** Nursery ~50 (no automatic onward right); **Reception 60**; **Year 7 180**; **Sixth form 150** (published academic entry criteria that do not exclude the school's own SEND/low-attainment leavers).
- **Banding, not distance-first.** Oversubscription is resolved by **fair banding** (a standardised test — CAT4-type — on application, allocating across ability bands) so the intake actually matches the comprehensive, ~45%-FSM6 planning assumption rather than skewing via a distance-only rule. Order: (1) looked-after/previously looked-after; (2) siblings; (3) fair-banding allocation with **straight-line distance as the in-band tiebreak**. EHCPs naming the school are admitted via LA consultation **outside** the oversubscription criteria.
- **Nursery→Reception:** nursery attendance **confers no Reception place**; families apply through the normal round (legally required; blocks backdoor selection).
- **Year 6→Year 7:** internal Year 6 pupils have a **guaranteed progression right** to Year 7, counting within the 180 PAN (60 internal, 120 external).
- **In-year admissions / backfill (Bet 7):** published commitment to **fill every casual vacancy in every year group**, no "full" gaming, including through the Fair Access Protocol.
- **Fair Access Protocol:** full participation, published.
- **EHCP magnet-effect bound (lawful form):** no "cap on consultations" is lawful — the LA may consult on any plan, and a school named in Section I of an EHCP must admit (Children and Families Act 2014, s.43). The bound operates through the two lawful channels in Section 7: a **written LA commissioning agreement fixing the ~24–36 funded base places**, plus **evidenced s.39(4) consultation responses** once genuinely full. This is how the 6× magnet effect is bounded without off-rolling.

## 5. Transition design headline

- **Enhanced transition for all external Year 7 joiners** (the two-thirds external majority): multiple summer-term visits, transition passports, structured SEND/attainment data handover with feeder primaries, and a summer induction bootcamp with crew/key-worker allocation before entry.
- **Extended programme for SEND/EHCP/vulnerable joiners:** spring-term visits, a named key worker allocated *before* entry, staggered/staged induction, and pre-transfer EHCP/ISP planning meetings.
- **Qualify the claim:** "removes the Year 6→7 cliff" applies to the **internal third only**; for the external two-thirds the mechanism is this transition programme, not all-through structure.
