# Department Operating Manual — Reader's Guide

This folder is the **operational layer** below [`blueprint/`](../blueprint/) — one level down from the whole-school design. The blueprint fixes the constants and the bets (structure, curriculum spine, teaching model, behaviour position, SEND model, coaching-over-appraisal). This folder is what those bets look like in the hands of a Head of Department, a class teacher, an ECT, a technician, a TA and a pupil, minute to minute, inside a real subject department.

**The science department is the worked example throughout** — it's Adam's actual remit (KS3 curriculum lead, TLR3), so every document grounds its policy in a concrete lab, a concrete lesson, a concrete Head of Science, rather than staying abstract. The model is written to generalise to any department in the school, not just science.
